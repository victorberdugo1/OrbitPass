Mission manifest

Target periods as publicly reported by NASA and operators (as of September 2026). Schedules change often and sources disagree; verify with the operator. Conflict risk: a reported mission within 150 km of the selected site whose target period overlaps the 30-day analysis window. Site coordinates are approximate and this is not an official deconfliction tool.

Operating window finder

Scans the 12 months after the selected date. Each candidate is a 14-day surface operation period starting 00:00 UTC, ranked by the mean of Sun and Earth availability. Approximate ephemeris; LOLA terrain horizon when available, spherical otherwise.

Data: JPL Horizons (ephemerides, queried live in the Docker version) and NASA Moon Trek (LRO WAC imagery tiles). Software: Three.js, an open-source 3D engine used only for rendering. Terrain horizons come from the Moon Trek LOLA elevation model (LRO_LOLA_DEM_Global_128ppd_v04, about 0.67 km per pixel, rays out to 200 km, Docker version).
