Sites

Mission manifest

Target periods as publicly reported by NASA and operators (as of September 2026). Schedules change often and sources disagree; verify with the operator. Conflict risk: a reported mission within 150 km of the selected site whose target period overlaps the 30-day analysis window. Site coordinates are approximate and this is not an official deconfliction tool.

Operating window finder

Scans the 12 months after the selected date. Each candidate is a 14-day surface operation period starting 00:00 UTC, ranked by the mean of Sun and Earth availability. Approximate ephemeris, spherical horizon, no terrain.
